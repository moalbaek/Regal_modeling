Per-component survival inputs. Non-responders use the Observation row. Shape k: <1 heavy tail (more long-term survivors), 1 = exponential, >1 accelerating. Applies to the plateau panel's cure-mixture components.
Evidence-informed default: 20%, with 10–30% as the primary sensitivity range. This is the frailty-linked share rejected, not every screen failure: biomarker mismatch, consent, logistics, treatment choice, and timing failures are excluded. Screening acts on baseline frailty, not on realized survival, so it enriches only as far as the frailty spread below allows.
Unobserved prognostic spread available to select on. At 0 no screening can enrich the cohort, and the entry window below barely enriches either. The evidence-informed 0.35 default is a central working assumption from observed AML risk-group heterogeneity; use 0.20–0.60 for sensitivity analysis.
Entry delay between CR2 and randomization, drawn Uniform[lower, upper]. Patients who die in this window never enrol, so survivors are enriched and the cure fraction rises. Set the upper bound to 0 to disable the layer; the lower bound is clamped to it. Both bounds are yours to set — the lower one is not a rounding detail: moving it from 1 to 0 shifts the BAT median by about half a month and the plateau rejection rate by several points. Assumption, not a protocol quantity: inclusion 7's ≥4-week rule runs from the last re-induction dose, not from CR2, and inclusion 8's 6 months is an upper bound only — the uniform shape between them is an analyst choice, and the lower bound is material. Death-only: the protocol also excludes patients who relapse in the window, but only overall survival is available to condition on. Whether that makes the enrichment shown here conservative is an assumption, not a derived bound — the true denominator is provably no larger, but the numerator moves with the joint relapse/death law too.
The component medians above are for all CR2 transplant-ineligible patients, but the trial enrols a healthier subset. Two pre-randomization layers model that, and neither one looks at post-randomization survival. (1) Eligibility screening acts on baseline frailty: unobserved prognostic spread of variance θ multiplies the uncured hazard, and screening rejects the frail preferentially. It rescales the uncured hazard only, so the cure fraction is unchanged and θ = 0 means no screening can enrich anything. (2) The CR2 → randomization window removes patients who do not survive to enrolment, which does raise the cure fraction, to c / E[S(D)]. Both are applied identically to both panels before the arm split, so neither biases the within-trial comparison. They act on the literature-sourced curves only — the BAT components and the non-responders on Observation. The fitted GPS responder families are not transformed by either layer: they are fitted to milestones from the enrolled cohort, so they already describe the post-selection population, and re-selecting them would double-count the screening. Superseded: earlier versions truncated the outcome instead, via min(1, S/(1−X)), which conditioned on each patient's realized death time — something no screening process can observe. Its flat early segment and its c/(1−X) cure inflation were artifacts of that, and both are gone.

Bounded no-GPS-cure alternative

In auto mode the GPS responder tail shape sG is a fitted parameter of the bounded no-GPS-cure alternative (alongside the GPS responder median mG) — the slider just displays the fitted value. Tick override to pin it and explore: sG < 1 = heavier tail; sG → 1 is exponential. sG is free to go genuinely heavy — that is what lets a no-cure Weibull try to mimic a plateau. If the fit lands on a parameter boundary, the panel reports a non-identified fit and withholds its rejection-rate estimate.
Note (plateau panel): BAT longevity comes from the component medians and the enrollment-selection slider — the milestone deceleration is explained by a healthier enrolled cohort. The fit's one free parameter is the GPS responder cure.

Anchored to SELLAS PRs (~20 by Apr 2022, 104 by Nov 2023, 126 by Apr 2024); those anchors pin the median to ~Q1–Q2 2023.
All-cause background mortality for a population mostly in their sixties, applied equally to both arms as a competing risk. It thins survival (incl. the plateau), brings the trigger forward, and — being non-differential — dilutes the treatment effect. ~2%/yr ≈ US ages 60–69; raise to stress-test.
Administrative censoring / dropout: patients who leave the study before dying are censored (no event), which slows event accrual and can stall the 80th-event trigger. 0 = complete follow-up; trials of this type run ~3–10%.
